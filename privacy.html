Privacy Policy

Urdu English Bible · Last updated: July 25, 2026

Urdu English Bible ("the app") is a bilingual Bible reader published by WordOnAir Labs. It is available as a website at urduenglishbible.wordonair.com and as an Android app on Google Play. This policy applies to both.

Summary

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

No accounts, no tracking

  • The app has no sign-up or login. We never ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information.
  • The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.
  • We do not use cookies.

Data stored on your device only

To make the app useful, some information is saved locally on your own device using your browser's standard storage (localStorage, IndexedDB, and the cache):

  • Your reading position and reading-plan progress
  • Highlights and notebook entries you create
  • Display preferences (theme, font size, margins)
  • Bible text you download for offline reading

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage (Android: Settings > Apps > Urdu English Bible > Storage > Clear data) or your browser's site data.

Network requests

The app fetches only the content it needs to display:

  • Bible text (Urdu Geo Version and World English Bible) from our public data repositories hosted on GitHub
  • Reading fonts from Google Fonts

These are ordinary content downloads. Like any web request, the servers involved (GitHub and Google Fonts) may see your IP address as part of serving the files; we do not receive, log, or have access to any of it.

Children

The app is a scripture reader suitable for general audiences. Since no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through wordonair.com.
